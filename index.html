Built for Independent Music.

Sisaiya Records Digital Media is an India-based independent music label focused on helping artists bring their music to digital audiences. From music distribution and digital delivery to artist-focused services and release support, our goal is simple — help independent music travel further.

We believe great music deserves a professional path to the world.

What We Do

Worldwide Music Distribution

Helping independent releases reach major digital music platforms and audiences worldwide.

Digital Music Delivery

Professional digital delivery designed to connect releases with today's major music ecosystems.

Artist Services

Artist-focused support for independent musicians building their identity and growing their digital presence.

Music Promotion

Supporting releases with digital-focused promotional strategies and audience discovery.

Release Management

Organized support around release planning, metadata, delivery and digital release workflows.

Rights & Copyright

Supporting artists and releases with a professional approach to rights, ownership and digital content management.
